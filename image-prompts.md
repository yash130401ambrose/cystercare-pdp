# Image prompts: "Support that goes beyond the bottle"

Four images for the right side of each card. Portrait **4:5**, at least 1200 × 1500 px.

**Rules for every image (from the brand photo guide)**
- Real-looking Indian women. Mix skin tones (include darker skin), body types and ages (20s to 40s).
- Visible, unedited skin: pores, acne, facial hair and dark circles are fine. No airbrushing or "glow" filters.
- Candid moments, natural daylight. No studio glam.
- Colour palette: blush pink, magenta (#E03192), deep plum (#24101F) and warm cream.
- No text, logos or watermarks in the image.

---

## 1. The formula (pink card)

Use a real product photo as the reference so the packaging stays exact.

> Premium product still life of a CysterCare supplement bottle with a few capsules spilled beside it, on a soft blush-pink stone surface. Around it are raw ingredients: fresh green amla, a few dried shatavari roots, sprigs of moringa leaves and a small dish of white inositol powder. Soft morning window light from the left, gentle shadows, shallow depth of field. Calm, minimal and clean, with lots of breathing room. Editorial wellness photography, 4:5 portrait.

## 2. The expert (dark plum card)

> A warm, candid photo of an Indian woman in her late 20s sitting at a wooden table at home, on a video call on her laptop with a friendly female nutritionist. The nutritionist is visible on screen, smiling and explaining something. The woman is taking notes in a notebook, with a cup of chai beside her. Evening light from a lamp, deep plum and warm amber tones. Natural skin with no retouching. Shot over her shoulder so both faces are visible. 4:5 portrait.

## 3. The coach (magenta card)

> A relaxed, candid photo of an Indian woman in her early 30s with medium-dark skin and natural curly hair, sitting cross-legged on a sofa, smiling as she reads something on her phone. She's in comfortable loungewear, with a bowl of fruit and a water bottle nearby. Bright, soft daylight with a magenta and blush-pink colour palette in the cushions and background. Natural skin with visible texture. Feels like a quiet "this actually helps" moment. 4:5 portrait.

## 4. The community (light pink card)

> A candid photo of four Indian women aged 22 to 40, with different body types and skin tones, laughing together around a low table with cups of chai and snacks. One wears a hijab and one has visible acne. They're leaning in as if sharing a story. Warm natural afternoon light, cream and blush-pink tones, real and unposed, like friends in someone's living room. 4:5 portrait.

---

**Tip:** if you generate these with AI, run each prompt a few times and pick the version where skin, hands and faces look most natural. If you shoot them for real instead, the same brief works as a shot list.
